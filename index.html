    🔍 How to Play Murdoku

    The Goal

    A murder has been committed. Your job is to figure out who did it. Place every suspect on the crime scene grid and identify who was alone with the victim.

    The Sudoku Rule

    Like Sudoku, each suspect must appear exactly once per row and exactly once per column. No two suspects can share a row or a column.

    The Murder Rule

    The victim is marked on the grid with a 💀. The murderer is the suspect who was placed in the same room (colored area) as the victim — they were alone together when the murder occurred.

    Using Clues

    • Read each clue carefully — they constrain which rows, columns, or rooms a suspect can occupy.
    • Click a clue to highlight the cells it refers to on the grid.
    • Use process of elimination to narrow down each suspect's exact position.

    Controls

    • Click a suspect → selects them (highlighted in the panel)
    • Click a cell → places the selected suspect there
    • Click an occupied cell → removes that suspect
    • Check Solution → verifies your answer
    • Reset → clears the board and starts over
