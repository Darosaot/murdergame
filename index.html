    🔍 How to Play Murdoku

    The Premise

    A murder has been committed. Your job is to identify the killer. Place every suspect on the crime scene grid and figure out who was alone with the victim.

    Rule 1 — The Sudoku Rule

    Each suspect must appear exactly once per row and exactly once per column. No two suspects can share a row or a column.

    Rule 2 — The Murder Rule

    The victim is marked 💀 on the grid. The murderer is the suspect placed in the same colored room as the victim — they were the only one there with them.

    Using Clues

    • Read each clue — it constrains a suspect's possible rows, columns, or rooms.
    • Click a clue to highlight the cells it refers to on the grid.
    • Use elimination to find each suspect's exact cell.
    • Column numbers (1–N) and row numbers (1–N) appear on the grid edges.

    Controls

    • Click a suspect card → selects them
    • Click an empty cell → places selected suspect there
    • Click an occupied cell → removes that suspect
    • Check Solution → verifies your placement
    • Undo → reverses the last placement or removal
    • Reset → clears all placements and saved progress
    • Your progress is saved automatically — reload the page and pick up where you left off
